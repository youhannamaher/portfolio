The Finance Tracker is a business-oriented financial management and analytics solution developed to centralize expense tracking, financial monitoring, and executive reporting into a clean and structured dashboard experience. The project was designed to transform manually managed financial records into an organized, interactive, and data-driven system that simplifies financial analysis and decision-making.

The solution allows users to manage and monitor financial activities through structured datasets, categorized transactions, KPI tracking, and dynamic visual dashboards. The platform focuses on improving financial visibility by converting raw spreadsheet data into meaningful insights through automated calculations, filtering systems, and visual analytics.

The project includes interactive dashboards displaying key financial indicators, expense distributions, category analysis, trend monitoring, and summarized business insights. The system was designed to help users quickly identify spending patterns, monitor financial performance, and make faster operational decisions without relying on manual calculations or fragmented Excel sheets.

Special attention was given to dashboard presentation, UI organization, and executive-level readability. The interface was redesigned to provide a modern and premium reporting experience with cleaner layouts, improved data hierarchy, and visually structured KPI sections to support management reporting and stakeholder presentations.

The project demonstrates skills in financial data structuring, dashboard design, data transformation, KPI visualization, and business reporting using tools such as Excel, Pivot Tables, Power Query, formulas, and advanced reporting techniques. It highlights how operational financial processes can be streamlined into a more scalable and professional analytical solution.
